वारी म्हणजे काय?

Every year, lakhs of warkaris walk for weeks from across Maharashtra to Pandharpur, singing abhangs composed by Sant Dnyaneshwar, Sant Tukaram, Sant Namdev and Sant Eknath — carrying their palkhis toward Lord Vitthal.

This radio is a small dindi of its own: a way to keep that rhythm — taal, mridang, and voice — going in the background of an ordinary day, wherever you are.
